Hey, my baby…

Happy birthday, Ayoka. ❤️

I honestly don’t even know where to begin, because how do you put seven years of love, memories, laughter, pain, growth, and everything in between into just a few words?

Seven years, baby. Look at us.

We’ve shared beautiful moments. We’ve had our difficult days, our misunderstandings, our moments of being apart, and somehow, we’ve found our way back to each other.

And when I think about everything we’ve been through, I realize that what we share is a story that will always mean something special to me.

Ayoka, you are not just someone I love. You are someone I’ve shared a part of my life with. Someone whose smile matters to me. Someone whose happiness I pray for, even in moments when I don’t always know the right words to say.

And today, I just want you to feel loved.

Not just through this website, not just through these words, but deep in your heart.

I want you to know that there’s someone here who is genuinely grateful that you were born. Someone who is grateful that, out of all the people in this world, our paths crossed.

My baby, as you begin another year of your life, I pray that God gives you the beautiful things your heart has been waiting for.

I pray for your peace, your happiness, your dreams, your health, and your success.

I pray that life becomes kinder to you. That you have more reasons to smile than to cry. That you never forget how precious you are, how much you deserve, and how beautiful it is to be you.

And I hope that when you look back on your life, this new chapter will be one of the chapters that makes you smile the most.

Ayoka…

If there’s one thing I want you to take away from this little message, it’s this:

Thank you for being part of my story.

Thank you for the memories we’ve made and for all the little moments that have become a part of who I am today.

Seven years is not just a number to me. It’s a collection of moments, lessons, feelings, and memories that I will always hold close to my heart.

And today, I’m celebrating you.

The beautiful woman you are. The woman you’re becoming. The dreams you’re chasing. And the life that is still waiting to unfold before you.

Happy birthday, my Ayoka.

My baby. My love.

I love you so much.

And I hope this little gift reminds you that, somewhere in this world, there is a heart that holds you very dearly.

Enjoy your day, baby.

You deserve to be celebrated.

Happy Birthday Ayoka ❤️
